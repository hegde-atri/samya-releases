#+TITLE: Changelog
#+AUTHOR: Atri Hegde

All notable changes to Samaya are recorded here, newest first. Each release
lists what is *New*, what is *Improved* and what is *Fixed*, and versions follow
[[https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html][Semantic Versioning]]. Each release is
tagged =vX.Y.Z=.

Until 1.0.0, a minor version may change how Samaya reads or writes Org files,
and a patch version never does.

* Unreleased

* 0.6.0 - 2026-10-14
** New
- *Keyboard shortcuts.* Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 switch to Today, Week and
  Focus, and N adds a task. Week takes B, D, 3, 5, W and M for its views, T
  for today and [ and ] to page; Focus takes Space, S and C. Press ? for a
  sheet of them all.

** Fixed
- Updating Samaya no longer resets your settings. A setting this version
  cannot read now falls back on its own, the rest are kept, and saving writes
  it back untouched for the version that understands it. Samaya also keeps a
  copy of the file as =desktop.toml.bak= before anything could be lost.

* 0.5.1 - 2026-10-14
** Fixed
- The Plane panel works with self-hosted Community Edition instances, which
  have only Plane's v1 API: Samaya lists each project's work items and applies
  the filters itself, where it said "not found" before.

* 0.5.0 - 2026-10-13
** New
- *Changelog.* Settings → Changelog lists what changed in each version, from
  the notes built into the app.
- *Plane.* Work items from saved searches across a workspace, on Plane Cloud
  or your own instance, signed in with an API token and planned by dragging
  one onto a day like the other integrations' items.

** Improved
- Each release on samaya-releases now carries its notes from the changelog,
  and the repository keeps a copy of the changelog itself.

* 0.4.0 - 2026-10-13
** New
- *Hidden events.* Right-click an event on the calendar to hide it, for the
  copies that block the same time in several calendars. Each day counts its
  hidden events at the top of its all-day row; click the count to show them
  dimmed, and right-click one to put it back on the calendar.

* 0.3.0 - 2026-10-12
** New
- *Azure DevOps.* Work items from saved WIQL searches across an organization,
  and pull requests awaiting your review or opened by you, signed in with =az=
  or a personal access token, planned by dragging one onto a day like GitHub's
  and Forgejo's.
- An ⓘ beside each integration's filters in Settings opens a guide to its
  filter language, with links to the full reference.

* 0.2.0 - 2026-10-11
** New
- Prebuilt x86_64 Linux binaries on
  [[https://git.hegdeatri.com/hegdeatri/samaya-releases/releases][samaya-releases]],
  with an installer that needs no compiler.
- *Forgejo.* Issues and pull requests from saved searches on a Forgejo or Gitea
  instance, signed in with =tea= or an access token, planned by dragging one
  onto a day like GitHub's.
- *Garmin.* Sign in from Settings → Garmin, and each day column greys out the
  night you slept and runs a bar down its left edge for each workout, with
  exact times on hover. Sleep can instead sit in that bar as a grey stretch.
- *stats.fm.* Name a public profile in Settings → stats.fm and its plays fill
  the same bar, with the track at that minute on hover.
- *The activity bar* draws workouts and plays as capsules in colours chosen in
  Settings, and Appearance sets their width and how overlaps draw: striped,
  side by side in lanes, or the shorter as a line down the longer. Days
  before yesterday are cached on disk and never fetched again.
- *Off the calendar.* Right-click a task's block, or its card, to unpin it
  from the calendar while keeping its day; Ctrl+Z puts it back. The task card
  lists its planned block with a ✕ that does the same.
- *Delete events.* Right-click one of your own events from a connected account
  to delete it at the provider, choosing on Google whether to email guests.
- A setting for whether replying to an invitation emails the organiser.

** Improved
- Completing a task in Focus saves the session and moves on to the next open
  task, until today has none left. The Exit button is gone; leaving Focus by
  any route saves the session.
- Events sit on a neutral fill behind a bar in their calendar's colour, and
  time-blocked tasks keep their channel tint without a border.

** Fixed
- A task trashed, unscheduled, moved to another day or edited in Emacs now
  deletes or moves its event on your calendar, instead of leaving it behind at
  the old time.

* 0.1.0 - 2026-10-05
The first release.

** New
- *Planning.* Today, a board of day columns, and day, three-day, week and month
  calendars. Tasks drag between days and onto time slots, and a block's bottom
  edge changes how long it takes, with Ctrl+Z to undo.
- *Channels and the backlog.* Each =.org= file is a channel, nestable one level
  deep, with its own colour. Tasks without a day sit in a channel's backlog
  and in the backlog panel, ready to drag onto a day.
- *Task cards.* Notes, subtasks, image attachments, priority, due dates and
  estimates, edited in place and written back to the task's headline.
- *Focus.* One task at a time with a stopwatch or pomodoro rounds, its subtasks
  and its notes. Each stretch focused is logged as a =CLOCK= entry; a pause
  under a minute keeps it one stretch. The time focused is tracked against the
  task's estimate and may run past it.
- *Focus on the calendar.* Focus sessions show as hatched blocks in their
  channel's colour, and drag and stretch to correct them. A task lists its
  sessions, and Today shows the day's focused total.
- *Google and Outlook calendars.* Several accounts, each calendar toggled and
  coloured on its own, synced into =calendar.org=. Event cards show the
  location, a *Join meeting* button, guests and description, answer
  invitations, and lengthen or shorten your own events.
- *Tasks on your calendar.* Time-blocked tasks can be copied to a chosen
  calendar and kept in step with their blocks.
- *GitHub.* Issues and pull requests from saved searches, planned by dragging
  one onto a day.
- *Org files as the source of truth.* Edits from Emacs show up straight away,
  and Samaya changes only the parts of a file it understands. Deleted tasks go
  to a trash they can be restored from.
- *Tray icon* with the current task and sync state, and a headless
  =samaya sync= command line for servers and systemd.
