#+TITLE: Changelog
#+AUTHOR: Atri Hegde

All notable changes to Samaya are recorded here, newest first. Each release
lists what is *New*, what is *Improved* and what is *Fixed*, and versions follow
[[https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html][Semantic Versioning]]. Each release is
tagged =vX.Y.Z=.

From 1.0.0, only a major version may change how Samaya reads or writes Org
files in a way that older versions cannot follow. *Planned* lists what is on
the way but not yet in a release.

* Planned
** Integrations
- *Slack status.* Your Slack status and notifications follow your focus
  sessions, events and time blocks, as Teams already does, with the same
  channel privacy deciding when a task's name is shown.

* Unreleased

* 1.9.0 - 2026-10-27
** New
- *Integrations can be turned off.* Each integration's page in Settings has a
  *Show in the rail* switch. Off, its mark leaves the rail and Samaya stops
  searching it, keeping its token and filters for when you turn it back on.
  Integrations start off, so turn on the ones you use after updating.

* 1.8.0 - 2026-10-26
** New
- *Update notices.* Once a day Samaya checks for a newer release. A dot by
  Settings and a new *Update available* page show what changed since your
  version, with a button that upgrades a Nix install or replaces a prebuilt
  binary once its signature checks out. A build from source gets a button
  that copies the install command.

** Improved
- Releases are signed with minisign. The installer checks the signature when
  minisign is installed, and the key is published beside the releases.

* 1.7.0 - 2026-10-26
** New
- *Teams status.* In Settings → Teams, choose a work or school Outlook account
  and what Teams shows while you focus, sit in an event or work a time block.
  By default focusing turns on Do not disturb with "Focusing on a task", a
  time block shows Busy, and events leave Teams to show In a meeting. Samaya
  clears only the status it set, and the status lapses by itself if Samaya
  quits.
- *Work and Personal channels.* Sort channels into Work and Personal in
  Settings → Channels; nested channels follow their parent. Every channel is
  private until you make a Work channel public, which asks first.
- *Task names in Teams.* Turn on Show the task's name to show "Focusing on:"
  and the task, only for a task in a public Work channel.

** Improved
- Outlook sign-in also asks to set your Teams status. Reconnect an account
  connected before 1.7.0 to use Teams.

* 1.6.0 - 2026-10-26
** New
- *Add a task from anywhere.* Press =A= on any Plan screen to bring up the
  composer at the foot of the page; on a channel it focuses the bar that is
  already there. Esc puts it away.
- *Quick-add tokens.* Type =#channel=, =@when=, =~estimate= and =!priority=
  in a new task, as in =Call mum @10am sunday ~20m #family !!=. Tokens are
  tinted as you type and a short list suggests channels, days and priorities;
  Tab or Enter takes one, and the arrows or Ctrl N and P move through it. The
  bar beneath shows the channel, day, time, estimate and priority it read, and
  a time adds the task to your calendar.

** Improved
- Estimates can be spelled out, as in =1hr10min=, =2hours= or =45mins=,
  wherever you type one.
- =N= on a channel opens the composer, and Ctrl Backspace in it deletes a whole
  word or token.

* 1.5.2 - 2026-10-26
** Improved
- A channel lists its done tasks last, under their own heading, the most
  recently completed first.

* 1.5.1 - 2026-10-26
** Improved
- Task blocks stay usable while they sync to your calendar, instead of greying
  out and locking. A change is sent a few seconds after your last edit, and
  never mid-drag, so moving or stretching a block again sends only the final
  time.

** Fixed
- An event deleted because its task was deleted or lost its planned time no
  longer reappears as a plain event until the next sync.

* 1.5.0 - 2026-10-26
** New
- *Focus in the background.* A started Focus session keeps running when you
  open another page or hide the window in the tray, and the sidebar shows its
  clock. Focusing another task from Today saves the running session first.

** Improved
- Focus saves its time and notes as it goes, every 30 seconds and whenever you
  leave the Focus page, so a crash loses at most that much.

* 1.4.0 - 2026-10-25
** Fixed
- A task's calendar event follows the task straight away however it changes,
  rather than after the next sync: taking its planned time off with ✕,
  moving, unplanning or deleting it from a menu, renaming it or editing its
  notes, or editing it in Emacs.
- Taking a block off the calendar just after dropping it no longer brings it
  back or leaves it stuck on the grid.
- Restoring a scheduled task from the trash, or undoing its delete with
  Ctrl+Z, puts its calendar event back. Its subtasks with their own planned
  times do not get theirs back yet; drag each onto the calendar again to
  recreate it.
- A task given a start but no end in Emacs moves its event, lasting its
  estimate as its block does.

* 1.3.1 - 2026-10-23
** Fixed
- Connections to calendars and integrations no longer trust certificates
  from intermediates that browsers have since distrusted.

* 1.3.0 - 2026-10-23
** New
- *Goals.* Year goals, and quarter goals beneath them with steps, habits and a
  weekly time budget, under *Plan → Goals*. Move between quarters and years,
  open a goal to edit it, and archive or delete goals; deleted ones wait in
  *Settings → Trash*. Goals live in =goals/<year>.org=, readable and editable
  in Emacs.
- *Habits.* A habit puts a task on each of its days this week in the goal's
  channel, shows its week as dots and its last eight weeks as a strip, and a
  habit task can be skipped from its right-click menu.
- *Measures.* A year goal can track a number toward a target, such as a race
  time or books read, with a chart and *Log* on its page.
- *Progress.* *Plan → Progress* lays the year out on one timeline: how far all
  goals have come against where they should be by now, then each goal's
  quarter goals, measure and habit weeks. *View* chooses what it shows and can
  add a summary to the Goals page and the sidebar.
- Tasks show their goal in place of their channel, the task editor links a
  task to a goal or habit, and completing a linked task shows its goal's
  progress, with a box to add the next step.
- The sidebar lists this quarter's goals and marks those that need a next
  step. *Settings → Appearance* can hide the list.

* 1.2.0 - 2026-10-20
** New
- New tasks and subtasks record when they were made, as a =CREATED=
  property in their Org file.

** Improved
- Unscheduled tasks are listed by priority, then oldest first, instead of in
  an arbitrary order. Tasks made before this release keep the order they have
  in their file.

* 1.1.0 - 2026-10-18
** New
- *Microsoft Planner panel.* The Planner tasks assigned to you, from saved
  filters, beside your plan; drag one onto a day to plan it. It reads through
  a work or school Outlook account connected in *Settings → Calendars*, chosen
  in *Settings → Microsoft Planner*. An account connected before this release
  needs reauthorizing once.

** Improved
- The Weekdays calendar view is now *Five days*: it always starts on today
  and ‹ › move it five days at a time.

* 1.0.0 - 2026-10-17
The first stable release. Samaya's Org files, settings and sync are now
settled, and later 1.x versions keep working with them.

** Improved
- Samaya is now built with the Rust 2024 edition, and needs Rust 1.90 or
  newer to build from source.

* 0.11.0 - 2026-10-17
** New
- *Select several tasks.* In a channel, Ctrl-click a task to add it to the
  selection and Shift-click to select a run of tasks, as in a file manager.
  Ctrl A selects them all and Esc clears the selection.
- *Make tasks subtasks.* With two or more tasks selected, a bar at the foot of
  the channel nests them under the first one, or under a task you search for,
  each keeping its own day and subtasks. The bar can also delete them all.
- Ctrl Z undoes deleting a task, from anywhere, and undoes nesting tasks,
  returning each to where it was.

** Improved
- A channel lists its planned days first and its unscheduled tasks after them.

* 0.10.1 - 2026-10-15
** Fixed
- Emoji in bold text, such as event titles on the calendar, show in colour
  instead of as faint outlines.

* 0.10.0 - 2026-10-15
** New
- *Choose your fonts.* Settings → Appearance can set the font for all text,
  and a separate monospace font for times, durations and shortcuts, from the
  fonts installed on your computer. Reset goes back to Fira Sans and Fira Mono.

* 0.9.0 - 2026-10-15
** New
- *Interface size.* Ctrl + and Ctrl − make the whole window bigger or
  smaller, from 80% to 150%, and Ctrl 0 resets it. Samaya remembers the size,
  which you can also change in Settings → Appearance.

* 0.8.0 - 2026-10-15
** New
- *Invitations show your answer.* On the day and week calendars, an
  invitation you have not answered yet is a dashed outline, one you answered
  Maybe is hatched, and one you declined is faded and struck through.
- A setting in Settings → Calendars to hide declined events.

** Fixed
- Answering an invitation updates its event on the calendar straight away,
  rather than after the next sync.

* 0.7.1 - 2026-10-15
** Fixed
- Samaya's secure connections to calendars and integrations use an updated
  TLS library, which fixes four security issues found in the previous one.

* 0.7.0 - 2026-10-15
** New
- =samaya --hidden= starts Samaya in the tray without opening its window, for
  starting it at login. Opening Samaya again shows the window.

* 0.6.0 - 2026-10-14
** New
- *Keyboard shortcuts.* Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 switch to Today, Week and
  Focus, and N adds a task. Week takes B, D, 3, 5, W and M for its views, T
  for today and [ and ] to page; Focus takes Space, S and C. Press ? for a
  sheet of them all.

** Fixed
- Updating Samaya no longer resets your settings. A setting this version
  cannot read now falls back on its own, the rest are kept, and saving writes
  it back untouched for the version that understands it. Samaya also keeps a
  copy of the file as =desktop.toml.bak= before anything could be lost.

* 0.5.1 - 2026-10-14
** Fixed
- The Plane panel works with self-hosted Community Edition instances, which
  have only Plane's v1 API: Samaya lists each project's work items and applies
  the filters itself, where it said "not found" before.

* 0.5.0 - 2026-10-13
** New
- *Changelog.* Settings → Changelog lists what changed in each version, from
  the notes built into the app.
- *Plane.* Work items from saved searches across a workspace, on Plane Cloud
  or your own instance, signed in with an API token and planned by dragging
  one onto a day like the other integrations' items.

** Improved
- Each release on samaya-releases now carries its notes from the changelog,
  and the repository keeps a copy of the changelog itself.

* 0.4.0 - 2026-10-13
** New
- *Hidden events.* Right-click an event on the calendar to hide it, for the
  copies that block the same time in several calendars. Each day counts its
  hidden events at the top of its all-day row; click the count to show them
  dimmed, and right-click one to put it back on the calendar.

* 0.3.0 - 2026-10-12
** New
- *Azure DevOps.* Work items from saved WIQL searches across an organization,
  and pull requests awaiting your review or opened by you, signed in with =az=
  or a personal access token, planned by dragging one onto a day like GitHub's
  and Forgejo's.
- An ⓘ beside each integration's filters in Settings opens a guide to its
  filter language, with links to the full reference.

* 0.2.0 - 2026-10-11
** New
- Prebuilt x86_64 Linux binaries on
  [[https://git.hegdeatri.com/hegdeatri/samaya-releases/releases][samaya-releases]],
  with an installer that needs no compiler.
- *Forgejo.* Issues and pull requests from saved searches on a Forgejo or Gitea
  instance, signed in with =tea= or an access token, planned by dragging one
  onto a day like GitHub's.
- *Garmin.* Sign in from Settings → Garmin, and each day column greys out the
  night you slept and runs a bar down its left edge for each workout, with
  exact times on hover. Sleep can instead sit in that bar as a grey stretch.
- *stats.fm.* Name a public profile in Settings → stats.fm and its plays fill
  the same bar, with the track at that minute on hover.
- *The activity bar* draws workouts and plays as capsules in colours chosen in
  Settings, and Appearance sets their width and how overlaps draw: striped,
  side by side in lanes, or the shorter as a line down the longer. Days
  before yesterday are cached on disk and never fetched again.
- *Off the calendar.* Right-click a task's block, or its card, to unpin it
  from the calendar while keeping its day; Ctrl+Z puts it back. The task card
  lists its planned block with a ✕ that does the same.
- *Delete events.* Right-click one of your own events from a connected account
  to delete it at the provider, choosing on Google whether to email guests.
- A setting for whether replying to an invitation emails the organiser.

** Improved
- Completing a task in Focus saves the session and moves on to the next open
  task, until today has none left. The Exit button is gone; leaving Focus by
  any route saves the session.
- Events sit on a neutral fill behind a bar in their calendar's colour, and
  time-blocked tasks keep their channel tint without a border.

** Fixed
- A task trashed, unscheduled, moved to another day or edited in Emacs now
  deletes or moves its event on your calendar, instead of leaving it behind at
  the old time.

* 0.1.0 - 2026-10-05
The first release.

** New
- *Planning.* Today, a board of day columns, and day, three-day, week and month
  calendars. Tasks drag between days and onto time slots, and a block's bottom
  edge changes how long it takes, with Ctrl+Z to undo.
- *Channels and the backlog.* Each =.org= file is a channel, nestable one level
  deep, with its own colour. Tasks without a day sit in a channel's backlog
  and in the backlog panel, ready to drag onto a day.
- *Task cards.* Notes, subtasks, image attachments, priority, due dates and
  estimates, edited in place and written back to the task's headline.
- *Focus.* One task at a time with a stopwatch or pomodoro rounds, its subtasks
  and its notes. Each stretch focused is logged as a =CLOCK= entry; a pause
  under a minute keeps it one stretch. The time focused is tracked against the
  task's estimate and may run past it.
- *Focus on the calendar.* Focus sessions show as hatched blocks in their
  channel's colour, and drag and stretch to correct them. A task lists its
  sessions, and Today shows the day's focused total.
- *Google and Outlook calendars.* Several accounts, each calendar toggled and
  coloured on its own, synced into =calendar.org=. Event cards show the
  location, a *Join meeting* button, guests and description, answer
  invitations, and lengthen or shorten your own events.
- *Tasks on your calendar.* Time-blocked tasks can be copied to a chosen
  calendar and kept in step with their blocks.
- *GitHub.* Issues and pull requests from saved searches, planned by dragging
  one onto a day.
- *Org files as the source of truth.* Edits from Emacs show up straight away,
  and Samaya changes only the parts of a file it understands. Deleted tasks go
  to a trash they can be restored from.
- *Tray icon* with the current task and sync state, and a headless
  =samaya sync= command line for servers and systemd.
