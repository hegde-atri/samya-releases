#+TITLE: Changelog
#+AUTHOR: Atri Hegde

All notable changes to Samaya are recorded here, newest first. Each release
lists what is *New*, what is *Improved* and what is *Fixed*, and versions follow
[[https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html][Semantic Versioning]]. Each release is
tagged =vX.Y.Z=.

From 1.0.0, only a major version may change how Samaya reads or writes Org
files in a way that older versions cannot follow. *Planned* lists what is on
the way but not yet in a release.

* Planned
** Planning
- *Agentic daily planning.* Tell Samaya about your work once and give each
  weekday a theme. Each morning, a short braindump becomes a planned day:
  tasks drafted from your integrations and backlog, ranked by the theme and
  your rules, and timeboxed around your calendar. Every change it makes is
  listed with its own undo, and the moves it was unsure of are left for you to
  decide.
- *Parallel tasks and timers.* Mark a task Parallel when it runs alongside
  other work, such as an agent fixing a bug or a build. Adjacent parallel
  tasks share their time on the calendar, and their timers run together,
  splitting the time evenly.

** Agents
- *Local MCP server.* Agents on your machine, such as Claude Code and Codex,
  can read and edit your tasks, plan days, start and stop timers and mark their
  own work Parallel, all written to your Org files like any other change.

** Integrations
- *Gmail.* Your Gmail in a panel, with the same conversations, actions and
  planning as Outlook mail.
- *Integration task details.* Tasks planned from GitHub, Forgejo, Plane and
  Planner show their item's details and let you edit them in place, as Azure
  DevOps tasks do.
- *Slack status.* Your Slack status and notifications follow your focus
  sessions, events and time blocks, as Teams already does, with the same
  channel privacy deciding when a task's name is shown.

* Unreleased

* 2.1.0 - 2026-11-25
Samaya 2.1 adds rituals: short guided routines to plan your day, end it and
review your week. *No breaking changes:* nothing new is written to your Org
files; a ritual's progress is kept in the planner's state file.
[[https://samaya.hegdeatri.com/news/v2-1][What's new in Samaya 2.1]].

** New
- *Daily planning.* Plan the day in four steps. Carry over decides each task
  left from yesterday: Today, Move to… or Delete. Pick adds goal steps and
  backlog tasks, with today's habits ticked. Fit lays the day beside its
  calendar and names every overlap. Commit starts the day. The header reads
  the day's planned time against its capacity throughout.
- *Shutdown.* End the day with what got done, then the leftovers, each moved
  to tomorrow, another day or the backlog, or deleted, then tomorrow's plan.
- *Weekly review.* Done compares the week's focus, tasks done, planned done
  and meetings with the week before. Goals shows each quarter goal's steps
  done since the last review, its logged time, habit days and health. Open
  decides the week's unfinished tasks, and Plan fills next week's days
  against their capacity. The review is due on its review day and reads
  Late after it.
- *Rituals in the sidebar.* The new RITUALS group shows each ritual's step
  while it runs, a check once done and a dot once due. It prompts but never
  opens a ritual by itself. On a narrow window, the rail's Rituals cell
  opens a menu of the three.
- G then D, S or R and the command palette open Daily planning, Shutdown and
  the weekly review, and Today's Plan day opens Daily planning. In a ritual,
  digits jump between steps, Ctrl ↵ takes the step's action, Alt ← goes back
  and Ctrl Z undoes a choice not yet written.
- Settings › Planning sets when Daily planning is due: at the first open of
  the day or at a time.
- A ritual keeps its step and choices when you leave it or quit, and each
  step's choices are written when you move on, with the usual Undo.

* 2.0.1 - 2026-11-25

** Improved
- Sign-in tokens are kept until they expire, not refreshed on every request.
- Week and Today redraw faster with many events and guests.
- A task card's channel menu lists an archived channel the task is still in,
  as the editor's does.
- An event card's link and calendar name are cut to fit its width.

** Fixed
- Day totals leave out tasks carried over from earlier days; their tooltip
  adds the time carried over.
- The add rows on boards and Today read channels, priorities and days as
  quick add does, instead of keeping them in the title.
- A change made while a calendar drop is still saving, such as Unpin, is no
  longer lost to the next one.

  its link in the notes, and a failed attach is reported.
- A locked keyring is no longer read as a missing token, which sent Azure
  DevOps to =az= and had Plane ask for its token again.
- Switching Teams accounts clears Samaya's status on the old one without
  touching the new account's own status.
- Adding a task, goal or step when the planner failed to open says so, and
  quick add keeps the task to retry.

* 2.0.0 - 2026-11-25
Samaya 2 redesigns the whole app on one design language. *No breaking
changes:* your Org files and their format are untouched, settings in
=desktop.toml= and =sync.toml= carry over, and connected calendars and
integrations keep working. Some keyboard shortcuts changed; see Improved.
[[https://samaya.hegdeatri.com/news/v2][What's new in Samaya 2]].

** New
- *A new design.* Every screen is rebuilt on one set of colours, type,
  spacing and components: the sidebar and its rail, the header, Today, Week,
  channels, Focus, the task editor, quick add, Goals, Progress, Analytics, the
  side panels, mail, Settings and the tray menu. Icons are Iconsax, and move
  slightly under the pointer.
- *Command palette.* Press Ctrl K to find a screen, channel, Settings page,
  task, event or goal, or to run an action. Empty, it lists your recent picks
  and what fits the moment, such as Back to Focus.
- *Keyboard everywhere.* Arrow keys move a ring over cards, rows, panels,
  calendar bookings, month days, goals and charts, and keys act on the ringed
  item: Space completes, F starts focus, T plans for today, D moves, C
  changes the channel, P sets the priority, Shift E the estimate, Del
  deletes and Shift F10 opens its menu. Every menu and dialog works from the
  keyboard.
- *Go to keys.* G then T, W, G, P, A, F or I opens Today, Week, Goals,
  Progress, Analytics, Focus or Inbox. Alt ← goes back through the screens and
  channels you visited.
- *Shortcut sheet.* Press =?= for every shortcut, with a filter.
- *Settings › Planning.* Set how much you plan to work on each weekday, your
  shutdown time, the weekly review day and the first day of the week. Today
  reads its planned time against the day's capacity, and Week marks a day
  only once it runs past it.
- *Settings overview and search.* Settings opens on an overview of each page
  and what needs attention. Search finds any setting on any page. Changed
  settings are marked, with Reset per setting and per page, and a list of
  everything you have changed.
- *One undo.* Ctrl Z undoes completing, deleting, moving and nesting tasks as
  well as calendar edits, from one history. A toast offers Undo after each.
  Undo refuses, with "Changed elsewhere", if the Org file changed since.
- *Quit safely.* Quitting waits for writes in flight, and asks before quitting
  while any change is still unsaved.

** Improved
- *Changed shortcuts.* Ctrl \ shows and hides the sidebar (was Ctrl B). In
  Week, =.= jumps to today (was T), 1 shows one day (was D), [ and ] page
  the period (was Shift ← →), and ← → move between days. In Focus, F starts
  and pauses (was Space), Space completes the task, and C is gone. In mail, Y
  archives (was E); E is now New event everywhere. In quick add, ↵ or a click
  takes a suggestion (was Tab), and Tab moves focus. Esc closes one layer per
  press, the latest first, and no longer leaves Settings: use Alt ← or Ctrl ,.
- A hidden sidebar folds to a narrow rail of icons instead of disappearing,
  and Focus folds it to the rail.
- Quick add is one card with suggestions inline. It opens under Add task on
  Today and in a channel, and beside a slot drawn on a calendar.
- The task editor is one list of properties: scheduled, estimate, logged,
  due, priority, channel and goal.
- Week names its period and view in the header and merges the filters into
  one menu. Month lays out six weeks, and a day's extra items drop below it.
  The time grids show the Day lane, open at the first booking, and zoom with
  Ctrl and the scroll wheel.
- Logged focus is drawn as a thin line in the day's margin, not as a block.
- Mail opens a conversation in a reading pane beside the panel, which takes
  the panel's place in a narrow window.
- The Backlog panel shows the backlog by default, with one filter menu.
- Analytics compares a period under way with as many days of the one before,
  such as 1–25 Nov against 1–25 Oct.
- Settings save when you let go of a control or press ↵, and each save
  replaces the file in one step. Corner radius is a choice of 0, 2, 4 or 6.
- A connected source is shown in Sources; the separate show switch is gone.
  Disconnect deletes the token and keeps the saved searches.
- Time blocks are coloured by channel or neutral; a saved Accent colour now
  reads as Neutral.

** Fixed
- A write that fails is held and marked on its task, with Retry in place,
  instead of one error for everything. A failed add keeps its text.
- Retrying an event never sends its invitations twice.
- An editor draft survives the task being edited outside Samaya, and is saved
  on quit along with the goal page's text.
- Links in tasks and mail open in the browser only when they are web links.
- Integration credentials are never sent over plain HTTP or to a redirect's
  host.

- *Changelog.* Settings → Changelog lists what changed in each version, from
  the notes built into the app.
- *Plane.* Work items from saved searches across a workspace, on Plane Cloud
  or your own instance, signed in with an API token and planned by dragging
  one onto a day like the other integrations' items.

** Improved
- Each release on samaya-releases now carries its notes from the changelog,
  and the repository keeps a copy of the changelog itself.

* 0.4.0 - 2026-10-13
** New
- *Hidden events.* Right-click an event on the calendar to hide it, for the
  copies that block the same time in several calendars. Each day counts its
  hidden events at the top of its all-day row; click the count to show them
  dimmed, and right-click one to put it back on the calendar.

* 0.3.0 - 2026-10-12
** New
- *Azure DevOps.* Work items from saved WIQL searches across an organization,
  and pull requests awaiting your review or opened by you, signed in with =az=
  or a personal access token, planned by dragging one onto a day like GitHub's
  and Forgejo's.
- An ⓘ beside each integration's filters in Settings opens a guide to its
  filter language, with links to the full reference.

* 0.2.0 - 2026-10-11
** New
- Prebuilt x86_64 Linux binaries on
  [[https://git.hegdeatri.com/hegdeatri/samaya-releases/releases][samaya-releases]],
  with an installer that needs no compiler.
- *Forgejo.* Issues and pull requests from saved searches on a Forgejo or Gitea
  instance, signed in with =tea= or an access token, planned by dragging one
  onto a day like GitHub's.
- *Garmin.* Sign in from Settings → Garmin, and each day column greys out the
  night you slept and runs a bar down its left edge for each workout, with
  exact times on hover. Sleep can instead sit in that bar as a grey stretch.
- *stats.fm.* Name a public profile in Settings → stats.fm and its plays fill
  the same bar, with the track at that minute on hover.
- *The activity bar* draws workouts and plays as capsules in colours chosen in
  Settings, and Appearance sets their width and how overlaps draw: striped,
  side by side in lanes, or the shorter as a line down the longer. Days
  before yesterday are cached on disk and never fetched again.
- *Off the calendar.* Right-click a task's block, or its card, to unpin it
  from the calendar while keeping its day; Ctrl+Z puts it back. The task card
  lists its planned block with a ✕ that does the same.
- *Delete events.* Right-click one of your own events from a connected account
  to delete it at the provider, choosing on Google whether to email guests.
- A setting for whether replying to an invitation emails the organiser.

** Improved
- Completing a task in Focus saves the session and moves on to the next open
  task, until today has none left. The Exit button is gone; leaving Focus by
  any route saves the session.
- Events sit on a neutral fill behind a bar in their calendar's colour, and
  time-blocked tasks keep their channel tint without a border.

** Fixed
- A task trashed, unscheduled, moved to another day or edited in Emacs now
  deletes or moves its event on your calendar, instead of leaving it behind at
  the old time.

* 0.1.0 - 2026-10-05
The first release.

** New
- *Planning.* Today, a board of day columns, and day, three-day, week and month
  calendars. Tasks drag between days and onto time slots, and a block's bottom
  edge changes how long it takes, with Ctrl+Z to undo.
- *Channels and the backlog.* Each =.org= file is a channel, nestable one level
  deep, with its own colour. Tasks without a day sit in a channel's backlog
  and in the backlog panel, ready to drag onto a day.
- *Task cards.* Notes, subtasks, image attachments, priority, due dates and
  estimates, edited in place and written back to the task's headline.
- *Focus.* One task at a time with a stopwatch or pomodoro rounds, its subtasks
  and its notes. Each stretch focused is logged as a =CLOCK= entry; a pause
  under a minute keeps it one stretch. The time focused is tracked against the
  task's estimate and may run past it.
- *Focus on the calendar.* Focus sessions show as hatched blocks in their
  channel's colour, and drag and stretch to correct them. A task lists its
  sessions, and Today shows the day's focused total.
- *Google and Outlook calendars.* Several accounts, each calendar toggled and
  coloured on its own, synced into =calendar.org=. Event cards show the
  location, a *Join meeting* button, guests and description, answer
  invitations, and lengthen or shorten your own events.
- *Tasks on your calendar.* Time-blocked tasks can be copied to a chosen
  calendar and kept in step with their blocks.
- *GitHub.* Issues and pull requests from saved searches, planned by dragging
  one onto a day.
- *Org files as the source of truth.* Edits from Emacs show up straight away,
  and Samaya changes only the parts of a file it understands. Deleted tasks go
  to a trash they can be restored from.
- *Tray icon* with the current task and sync state, and a headless
  =samaya sync= command line for servers and systemd.
