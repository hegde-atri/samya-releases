Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026.

Samaya is a desktop app that runs on your computer. It has no server and no account, and its developer receives none of your data.

What Samaya keeps

Your tasks, goals, notes and focus time live in Org files in a folder you choose. Synced calendar events go to calendar.org in the same folder. Samaya keeps its settings and a cache of synced data under ~/.config/samaya and ~/.local/share/samaya (or their Windows equivalents). Sign-ins and client secrets go to your system's credential store, or, where there is none, to files only you can read.

All of it stays on your computer. Deleting those files removes it.

Google user data

When you connect a Google account, Samaya asks for these permissions and uses them only as follows:

PermissionWhat Samaya uses it for
openidRecognizing the account, so a re-authorization can't silently switch to another one
calendar.readonlyListing your calendars and reading their events, to show them beside your tasks and in calendar.org
calendar.eventsCreating and changing the events you make or schedule in Samaya, and replying to invitations
contacts.readonly, contacts.other.readonly, directory.readonlyFinding profile pictures of your events' guests, to show on event cards

Samaya talks to Google's APIs directly from your computer. Data it receives from Google is kept only on your computer, as described above. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it.

Samaya's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting an account, in Settings → Calendars or with samaya sync disconnect, revokes Samaya's access at Google and deletes the account's sign-in and cache from your computer. You can also revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other services

The same applies to Microsoft (Outlook, Teams and Planner), GitHub, Forgejo, Azure DevOps and Plane. Samaya connects to them directly, using the sign-ins you give it, only to sync what you set up. What it receives stays on your computer.

Update checks

Once a day, Samaya asks git.hegdeatri.com whether a newer release is out. The request carries nothing about you beyond what any web request does, such as your IP address.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on Vercel, which may log requests as described in its privacy policy.

Changes

Any change to this policy is posted on this page, with a new effective date.
