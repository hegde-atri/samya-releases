Welcome to Samaya

Samaya is a desktop planner for Linux and Windows. Your tasks, goals and focus time live in plain Org files in a folder you choose, beside your Google and Outlook calendars and the issues you work on. There is no account and no server: edit the same files in Emacs, and Samaya shows the change.

Install

Linux (x86_64) and Windows (x64) builds are on samaya-releases. On Linux, one command installs it: 

curl -fsSL https://git.hegdeatri.com/hegdeatri/samaya-releases/raw/branch/main/install.sh | sh

Getting started covers Windows, Nix, updates and verifying a download.

Point it at your Org folder

  1. Make a folder for your tasks, such as ~/org. An empty one is fine, and so is a folder you already use with Emacs.

  2. Open Settings → Org files and choose it. Each .org file in it is a channel, such as work.org or health.org.

  3. Name, nest and colour channels in Settings → Channels.

Samaya changes only the parts of a file it understands. See Org files for how tasks, goals and clock entries map to Org.

Connect calendars and sources

Settings → All sources lists what is connected and what is available.

  • Calendars. Connect, choose Google or Outlook, name the account and sign in. Events sync into calendar.org. See Google Calendar and Outlook calendars.

  • Work items. GitHub, Forgejo, Azure DevOps, Plane and Microsoft Planner show saved searches in a side panel. Drag an item onto a day to plan it.

  • Mail and status. Outlook mail opens in a side panel, and Microsoft Teams status follows your focus.

A tour of the screens

Today

The day's tasks, in order, with the day's calendar beside them. The header reads the planned time against the day's capacity. Press N to add a task.

Week

Seven day columns as a board, or a time grid of one, three, five or seven days, or a month. Drag tasks between days and onto time slots. Press B, 1, 3, 5, W or M to switch views.

Focus

One task at a time, with a stopwatch or pomodoro rounds, its subtasks and notes. Each stretch is logged as an Org clock entry.

Goals

Year and quarter goals with steps, habits and a weekly time budget. Each goal shows its health and next step.

Progress

Every goal's pace across the year: ahead, on pace or slipping.

Analytics

Focus by channel, day and hour, and tasks done and created, for a week, month, quarter or year.

Channels

One channel's tasks, grouped by day, with the backlog below. Pick one in the sidebar.

Settings

Open them with Ctrl , or from the foot of the sidebar. Settings opens on an overview, and its search finds any setting. Changes save as you make them.

Settings are kept in ~/.config/samaya on Linux and %APPDATA%\samaya on Windows: desktop.toml for the app and sync.toml for calendar sync. Passwords and tokens are kept in the system keyring or Windows Credential Manager, never in those files.
