Plan

Your week, on one screen.

Day columns or a calendar from one day to a month. Drag tasks between days and onto time slots, and stretch a block to change how long it takes. Your Google and Outlook events sit beside them.

Goals

Year goals, worked a week at a time.

Quarter goals with steps, habits and a weekly time budget. Habits put their days on your week, and Progress shows whether each goal is ahead, on pace or behind.

Focus

One task at a time.

A stopwatch or pomodoro rounds, with the task's subtasks and notes. Every stretch is logged as an Org clock entry, and can set your Teams status while you work.

Plain text

Org files are the source of truth.

Each file is a channel. Edit in Emacs and Samaya shows it straight away; it changes only the parts of a file it understands, and never overwrites your edits. How tasks map to Org →

Integrations

Your work queue, beside your plan.

Issues, pull requests and work items from saved searches. Drag one onto a day to plan it. Calendars sync from Google and Outlook.

Get it

Install in a minute.

Free for Linux and Windows. Samaya offers each new release in Settings, so you install it once.

curl -fsSL https://git.hegdeatri.com/hegdeatri/samaya-releases/raw/branch/main/install.sh | sh

x86_64, glibc 2.36 or newer. Verifies the checksum, and the signature when minisign is installed.

Download samaya-2.4.1-x86_64-windows.zip

Extract it and open samaya.exe. No installer or admin rights needed.
