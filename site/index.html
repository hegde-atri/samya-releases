A calm daily planner over your own Org files.

Plan the day, focus on one task, and keep everything in plain text you can also edit in Emacs. No account, no server.

Download Docs
Pre-beta · Linux x86_64 · Windows x64

Plan your day

Today, a board of days, and day to month calendars. Drag tasks onto time slots.

Focus

One task at a time, with a stopwatch or pomodoros, logged as Org clock entries.

Goals

Year and quarter goals with steps, habits and a weekly time budget.

Calendars

Google and Outlook, several accounts, with invitations answered in place.

Your work queue

GitHub, Forgejo, Azure DevOps, Plane and Microsoft Planner, beside your plan.

Plain Org files

Edits from Emacs show up straight away. Samaya changes only what it understands.

Install on Linux

curl -fsSL https://git.hegdeatri.com/hegdeatri/samaya-releases/raw/branch/main/install.sh | sh
