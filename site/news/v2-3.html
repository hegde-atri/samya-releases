Keyboard first

This one's mostly for the keyboard. I wanted to get through Goals, Progress and Analytics without reaching for the mouse, so now you can move around them with ↑ ↓ and press ↵ to open things. You can also type straight into the editor's pickers, like "fri 9:30" for when a task is scheduled.

Download Changelog

No breaking changes

  • Org files and their format haven't changed.

You'll get the update in Settings › Update, or you can run the installer again.

GoalsProgressAnalyticsScheduledAlso

Goals

Use ↑ ↓ to move through your goals, their habit days and a goal's steps. ↵ opens the goal you're on, and L logs a value for a measure you track by hand.

Progress

Same idea on Progress, for goals, measures and habits. When you land on a habit, its week pops up right over its cell. ← → fold a year goal open or closed, and on a habit they step through the weeks.

Analytics

Click the period name to pick a month, quarter or year. On Week you get a small month calendar instead. ↑ ↓ move through the channels, and ↵ narrows Analytics down to the one you're on.

Scheduled

In the editor's Scheduled picker you can now type something like "fri 9:30", the same way you would in quick add. It shows you the day and time it understood, tells you if that overlaps anything, and ↵ saves it. The Goal picker filters as you type, too.

Also in 2.3

  • Shift ↑ ↓ extends your selection in a channel, like Shift-click does. And "N hidden" now unfolds every group that's hiding something you selected.
  • On Today's calendar and the Week grids, a block uses the full width when nothing else is on at the same time.
  • Esc clears what you typed in a picker first, and only closes it on the next press.

Get it

Downloads are on samaya-releases, and there's a full list of changes in the changelog. The new setup for first-time users came in Samaya 2.2, if you missed it.
