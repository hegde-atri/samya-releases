Keyboard first

Samaya 2.3 takes the ring to Goals, Progress and Analytics, so you can walk them with ↑ ↓ and act with ↵, and lets you type into the editor's pickers.

Download Changelog

No breaking changes

  • Org files and their format are untouched.

Samaya offers the update in Settings › Update; or run the installer again.

GoalsProgressAnalyticsScheduledAlso

Goals

↑ ↓ move one ring over the goals, their habit days and a goal's steps. ↵ opens the ringed goal and L logs a measure kept by hand.

Progress

The ring walks goals, measures and habits. A ringed habit reads its week out over its own cell, and ← → fold a year goal or step the week.

Analytics

The period name opens a picker of months, quarters or years, or a mini month for Week. ↑ ↓ ring the channels and ↵ narrows to the ringed one.

Scheduled

Type "fri 9:30" as you would in quick add. Scheduled shows the day and time it reads, with anything it overlaps, and writes it on ↵. The Goal picker filters as you type.

Also in 2.3

  • Shift ↑ ↓ extends a channel's selection, as Shift-click does, and "N hidden" unfolds every group hiding a pick.
  • On Today's calendar and Week's grids, a block takes the full width where it runs alone.
  • Esc clears a picker's typed text before it closes the picker.
