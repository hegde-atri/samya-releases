Samaya 2

Every screen is rebuilt on one design language: one set of colours, type, spacing and components, with Iconsax icons. Samaya 2 adds a command palette, a capacity for each weekday, and keyboard control of every card, panel, menu and dialog.

Download v2.0.0 Changelog

No breaking changes

  • Org files and their format are untouched.
  • Settings in desktop.toml and sync.toml carry over. A value you saved wins over a new default.
  • Calendars, integrations and accounts keep working, with no new sign-in.

Some keyboard shortcuts changed: see Changed shortcuts. Samaya offers the update in Settings › Update; or run the installer again.

Sidebar and header

The sidebar lists Plan and your channels, with Search and Focus on top and sync state at its foot. Ctrl \ folds it to a rail of icons; Focus folds it for you. Every screen shares one header: its title, period, view and panel toggles.

Today

The day's tasks beside its calendar. The header reads planned time against the day's capacity. Add task opens quick add in place, and every card works from the keyboard.

Week

The header names the period and the view; one menu holds the filters. The board's columns are a fixed width, the time grids open at the first booking and zoom with Ctrl and the wheel, and Month shows six weeks.

Focus

The task, its timer and its subtasks in one column. F starts and pauses, Space completes the task, and End focus saves the session without completing it.

Task editor and quick add

The editor is one list of properties: scheduled, estimate, logged, due, priority, channel and goal. Quick add is one card with its suggestions inline, opened under Add task or beside a slot drawn on a calendar.

Version 1

Goals

Goals in fixed columns: logged time against the plan, this week's habits, the next step and health. The goal page shares the same parts.

Progress

Each goal's pace across the year, with today's line in the accent. Arrow keys step through habit days, weeks and bars.

Analytics

Focus by day, channel and hour. A period under way is compared with as many days of the one before, such as 1–25 Nov against 1–25 Oct.

Side panels and mail

Backlog, Sources and Outlook mail share one header and one-line rows. Shift B and Shift S put the keyboard ring on a panel's first row. Mail opens a conversation in a reading pane beside the panel, and Y archives it.

Settings

Settings opens on an overview of each page and what needs attention. The nav is grouped into Preferences, Sources, Data and About; a connected source is a row in Sources.

Version 1

Tray

The tray menu is reordered to match the app, under the new Samaya marks.

New

Command palette

Ctrl K finds a screen, channel, Settings page, task, event or goal, or runs an action. Empty, it lists your recent picks and what fits the moment.

Settings › Planning

Capacity per weekday, the shutdown time, the weekly review day and the first day of the week. Today and Week read their totals against each day's capacity.

Keyboard everywhere

Arrow keys move one ring over cards, rows, panels, bookings, month days, goals and charts. Every menu and dialog works from the keyboard, and each menu row shows its key. Press ? for the shortcut sheet, with a filter.

Settings search and reset

Search finds any setting on any page. Changed settings are marked, with Reset per setting and per page.

One undo

Completing, deleting, moving and nesting tasks show one toast with Undo, and Ctrl Z undoes them from one history with calendar edits. Undo refuses if the Org file changed since.

Safer writes

A write that fails is held, marked on its task and retried in place; the sidebar foot reads “Not saved · Retry”. Quitting waits for writes in flight and asks before dropping a held one. Editor drafts survive edits made outside Samaya.
