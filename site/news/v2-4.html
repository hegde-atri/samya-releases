See it before you pick it

Samaya 2.4 draws the calendar's look in Settings as pictures, so each choice shows what it does. It adds a choice of where logged focus draws, and gives task blocks their channel's colour.

Download Changelog

No breaking changes

  • Org files and their format are untouched.
  • Settings you saved carry over. Only new setups start on Side by side.

The sidebar now hides with Ctrl B, in place of Ctrl \. Samaya offers the update in Settings › Update; or run the installer again.

Settings

Scheduling shows Time-block colour, Logged focus and Overlapping activities as true-size pictures of a calendar, drawn with your other choices in force. Click one to pick it. Activity bar width sets the bar beside a live strip, and Garmin's Sleep on the calendar is pictured too.

Logged focus

Beside block keeps a session as a hatched box beside its task's block. The new Inside block fills the logged stretch into the block itself.

Channel colours

With Time-block colour By channel, a task block carries its channel's colour bar, as an event carries its calendar's.

Mail

Read conversations are muted, so the unread ones stand out. Archiving or deleting the one you are reading opens the next.

Day lane

Week's Day lane scrolls again when a day holds more than fits. A day with hidden events leads it with their count, which shows them.

Also in 2.4

  • The activity bar's Stripes are solid bands across the whole bar, and Nested one unbroken line down the longer activity.
  • Settings' Back button and Ctrl , return to the screen Settings was opened from.
  • A New goal that failed to save is no longer lost on quit: quitting asks first.
  • Logged focus draws as hatched boxes again.
