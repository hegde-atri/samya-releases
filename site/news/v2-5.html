Where the hours went

I wanted to actually see where my week goes. Was it deep work, or was it meetings again? So Analytics has a new Time view. It splits your week into focus, meetings and everything else on your calendar, and shows the time blocks you planned next to the focus you actually logged.

Download Changelog

No breaking changes

  • Org files and their format haven't changed.
  • Settings you've saved stay as they are. Analytics still opens on Tasks until you switch it.

You'll get the update in Settings › Update, or you can just run the installer again.

Time

The tiles at the top show your logged focus, your calendar meetings and your other calendar events, and how much of the week each one took. If a meeting overlaps a focus session, it only gets counted once, so the totals add up to a real week. Focus wins, then meetings, then everything else. Declined, cancelled and all-day events don't count, and neither do calendars you've hidden.

With Focus selected, the time you blocked out shows as an outline on each bar, and the focus you logged fills it in. You can see at a glance how much of the plan actually happened. The Blocks kept tile puts a number on it: how much of your blocked time you really spent on the task it was for.

Meetings

Switch to Meetings or Events and you get a table of every one of them. Booked is how long it sat on your calendar. Counted is what's left once your focus and anything booked before it are taken out. Hover over a row to compare it with the period before. The heat map underneath shows which days and hours all that time landed on.

Tasks or Time

There's a switch at the top of Analytics to flip between Tasks and Time (or press V). Whatever week, month or channel you're looking at stays put when you switch, and it remembers which one you were on last.

Month and quarter

Look at a month and you get a bar for each day. A quarter gives you a bar for each week. Narrow it down to one channel and the chart, the outlines and the heat map all follow.

I also changed how a period that isn't over yet gets compared, on Tasks as well. Half of today used to be up against the whole of the same day last time. Now 1–27 November up to right now is compared with 1–27 October up to the same time of day.

Also in 2.5

  • The Meetings number in the weekly review used to count every event. Now it only counts actual meetings, meaning events with another guest or a join link.
