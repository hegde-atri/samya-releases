Where the hours went

Samaya 2.5 adds a Time view to Analytics. It sets your logged focus beside your calendar's meetings and events, counts each minute once, and draws your time blocks as the plan that focus fills.

Download Changelog

No breaking changes

  • Org files and their format are untouched.
  • Settings you saved carry over. Analytics opens on Tasks until you switch.

Samaya offers the update in Settings › Update; or run the installer again.

TimeMeetingsTasks or TimeMonth and quarterAlso

Time

Three tiles split the week into logged focus, calendar meetings and other calendar events, each with its share. Every minute counts once: focus first, then meetings, then events. Declined, cancelled and all-day events, and calendars you hide, are left out.

With Focus picked, each bar outlines the time you blocked, and logged focus fills it. Blocks kept says how much of your blocks' time went to their own task.

Meetings

Pick Meetings or Events and the table lists each one with the time it booked beside the time it counted, after focus and earlier bookings. Hover a row for its readout against the period before. The heat map shows the weekdays and hours that time took.

Tasks or Time

The view switch in Analytics' header turns between Tasks and Time; V opens it. The period, span and channel carry across, and Samaya remembers the view you left.

Month and quarter

A month draws a bar a day and a quarter a bar a week. Narrow to a channel and the chart, its outline and the heat map follow it. A period still under way compares with the same moment in the period before, on Tasks too: 1–27 November to now against 1–27 October to the same time.

Also in 2.5

  • The weekly review's Meetings figure counts only meetings, events with another guest or a join link, instead of every event.
