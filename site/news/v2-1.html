Rituals

I wanted a proper routine for starting the day, ending it, and looking back at the week, instead of doing it all in my head. So there are three of them now. You plan the day in the morning, shut it down in the evening, and review the week at the end of it. Each one is a few short steps where you decide one thing at a time, and everything gets written to your Org files as you go.

Download Changelog

No breaking changes

  • Org files and their format haven't changed. Everything a ritual does is a normal move, skip or delete, and you can undo each one.
  • Ritual progress (which step you're on and any choices that aren't written yet) is kept in the planner's state file. You can leave or even quit, and pick up where you left off.

You'll get the update in Settings › Update, or you can run the installer again.

Daily planning

It starts with Carry over. For each task left from yesterday, you choose Today, Move to… or Delete. The whole way through, the header shows how much you've planned against how much time you have that day.

In Pick you add goal steps and tasks from your backlog. Today's habits are already ticked for you.

Fit puts your day next to your calendar and points out anything that overlaps. Drag a task to a time, or press Ctrl → to push it to tomorrow. Commit starts the day. If something still overlaps, it'll offer to take you Back to Fit first.

Shutdown

At the end of the day you see what got done first, then what's left over. Each leftover goes to tomorrow, another day or the backlog, or you delete it. The header shows how full tomorrow is getting while you do this. Last up is tomorrow's plan.

Weekly review

Done compares this week with the one before: your focus time, tasks done, planned tasks done and meetings. Then it lists what you got done in each channel.

Goals goes through each quarter goal. You see the steps you've finished since your last review, the time you logged and the habit days you hit that week, how the goal is doing, and what's next.

Open is where you deal with whatever's still unfinished from the week. Then Plan lays out next week's days against how much time each one has. Drag a goal's next step onto a day, or press D.

In the sidebar

The rituals live in the sidebar under RITUALS. While one is running you can see which step you're on. It gets a check when you're done and a dot when it's due, and the weekly review says Late once its day has passed. Samaya will remind you, but it never opens a ritual on its own. I didn't want it taking over the screen the moment I open the app. If your window is narrow, the Rituals icon in the rail opens a menu with all three.

In Settings › Planning you choose when Daily planning is due, either the first time you open the app that day or at a set time. The shutdown time and the review day are there too.

Keys

You can do every step from the keyboard. Press ? in the app to see all the shortcuts.

KeyDoes
G then D, S, ROpen Daily planning, Shutdown, the weekly review (also in the palette, Ctrl K)
1–4Jump to a step you reached
Ctrl ↵The step's action: Next, Commit, Finish
Alt ←Back a step
↑ ↓Move between rows
T, D, DelToday, Move to…, Delete the row's task

Ctrl ZUndo a choice that isn't written yet, then the usual Undo
