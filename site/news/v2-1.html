Rituals

Samaya 2.1 adds three short, guided routines: plan the day in the morning, shut it down in the evening, and review the week. Each is a few steps, one decision per row, written to your Org files as you go.

Download Changelog

No breaking changes

  • Org files and their format are untouched. A ritual's choices are ordinary moves, skips and deletes, each with Undo.
  • Ritual progress, the step you are on and the choices not yet written, lives in the planner's state file, so it survives leaving and quitting.

Samaya offers the update in Settings › Update; or run the installer again.

Daily planning

Carry over decides each task left from yesterday: Today, Move to… or Delete. The header reads the day's planned time against its capacity at every step.

Pick adds goal steps and backlog tasks; today's habits come ticked.

Fit lays the day beside its calendar and names every overlap. Drag a task to a time, or Ctrl → to move it to tomorrow. Commit starts the day; while something overlaps, it offers Back to Fit first.

Shutdown

End the day with what got done, then the leftovers, each moved to tomorrow, another day or the backlog, or deleted. The header reads tomorrow's load, so you see it fill as you go. Last, tomorrow's plan.

Weekly review

Done compares the week's focus, tasks done, planned done and meetings with the week before, then lists what got done by channel.

Goals shows each quarter goal's steps done since the last review, its logged time and habit days that week, its health and its next step.

Open decides the week's unfinished tasks. Plan fills next week's days against their capacity: drag a goal's next step onto a day, or press D.

In the sidebar, never in the way

The RITUALS group shows each ritual's step while it runs, a check once done and a dot once due; the weekly review reads Late after its day. Samaya prompts but never opens a ritual by itself. On a narrow window, the rail's Rituals cell opens a menu of the three.

Settings › Planning sets when Daily planning is due, at the first open of the day or at a time, beside the shutdown time and the review day.
